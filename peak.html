Peak Mnot

How to play

WASD or arrows
move; right always means onwards
Space
jump; a running jump clears about a metre
Shift
careful steps: stop at an edge, grip on ice
A D on a plank or rope
lean against the wobble
drag, wheel, M
look around, zoom, sound

Climb from base camp to the summit, fast: every fall or falling rock dents the can and the fourth dent flattens it, each checkpoint flag pops the dents back out, loose ledges crumble, ice slides and wind gaps blow on a beat, so look before you leap.
